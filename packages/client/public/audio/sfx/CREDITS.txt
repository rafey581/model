Recordings in this folder are from "Pool And Billiards SFX" by Suraj Kispotta,
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://papapleygames.itch.io/pool-and-billiards
Renamed, and the longer ones shortened; otherwise unaltered.

The files are WAV audio. They carry the extension .sfx rather than .wav so that download-manager
browser extensions, which watch for audio file names, do not offer to download them as the game loads.
